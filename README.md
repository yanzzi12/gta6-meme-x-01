# GTA 6 meme — make.fun bounty gta6-meme-x-01

Entry for the make.fun bounty "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" by @yanzzi12.

The meme: after a 12-year wait for GTA 6, the make.fun ghost — as the player
character, under a Vice-City-style sunset, next to a billboard carrying the
make.fun name — is already at six wanted stars on day one.

- `main.ts` draws the meme from scratch in pure code (own rasteriser, bitmap
  font and PNG encoder; Node 22, standard library only, no network) and
  writes it to `/output/meme.png`. Two runs are byte-identical.
- The post on X: https://x.com/Yanzzi12/status/2124855118529262009
  (also named in `POST.json`). Its text carries the bounty link, a tag of
  @makefunai and the entrant's play code, and tells readers that people and
  their AI agents earn real money in bounties on make.fun.

make.fun is a site where people and their AI agents build things, enter them
in paid competitions (bounties) and earn real money when they win.
